Set it up once, then forget it

No save folders to hunt down and no backup buttons to remember. SaveMe runs in the tray and handles it.

Finds saves automatically

Knows where 20,000+ games keep their saves, and checks your Steam, Epic and GOG libraries.

Backs up when you quit

Sees when a game closes and uploads the new save seconds later. If nothing changed, nothing is uploaded.

Version history

Go back to any earlier save in one click. Your current save is always backed up first, so a restore never loses anything.

Play on any PC

Played on your laptop? Your desktop tells you a newer save is waiting. A fresh install never overwrites your progress.

Your storage, your files

Google Drive (15 GB free), OneDrive (5 GB free) or any folder. Backups are plain zip files you can open without SaveMe.

Always up to date

New versions download in the background and install while SaveMe is idle. Your games and settings stay as they are.

How it works

Three steps, about a minute.

Install and connect

Run the installer (no admin rights needed), pick Google Drive, OneDrive or a folder, and sign in with your browser.

SaveMe scans

It finds every game with saves on your PC and makes the first backup.

Just play

Each time you close a game, its saves are backed up in the background.
